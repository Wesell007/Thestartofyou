## Claim discipline

- No success-rate comparison is made between IVF and ICSI, or between fresh and frozen transfer.
- No storage time limits or statutory figures are stated, because HFEA storage rules change; the guide signposts HFEA patient information and the reader's own clinic consent forms instead.
- Embryo grading is described as a record of appearance at a moment in time, not a prediction.
- Every clinical statement is attributable to one of the verified sources above; anything that could not be supported was cut rather than softened.
