# Phase 36C — Pregnancy claim resolution evidence

Scope: the 14 unsupported medical/numerical claims identified in Phase 36B (12 in the six label-only-source articles, 2 in articles with no source records), plus the two audited "completely normal" implantation-bleeding certainty phrasings, which are resolved inside claim C9A/C9B below rather than added to the total.

Rules applied: no invented URLs, no inferring evidence from publisher similarity, no substituting an unrelated source, no invented thresholds or clinical timings, no added medical certainty. Where no exact repository provenance existed, the numerical precision was removed or the assertion safely softened.
