# TSOY homepage brand film: shot plan (written before generation)

Date: 24 October 2026. Status: AI-GENERATED BRAND-FILM CONCEPT / DESIGN PROTOTYPE material only. Nothing here is production footage.

## Purpose

A 10 second, silent, seamless background loop behind real HTML copy (logo, headline, deck, one primary action). The film is atmosphere. It carries no meaning that the page needs; the headline must read on the poster alone and on a plain paper fallback.

## Global rules for all three concepts

- Natural light only; late-afternoon or morning; muted warm palette (cream, straw, sage, peach, soft clay). No teal-orange grade, no bleach.
- Restrained camera: locked-off or slow dolly or slow push; no whip pans, no drone sweeps, no exaggerated slow motion.
- Real domestic detail; hands, writing, movement, small family moments. Observational, not posed. Diverse real-looking people, no influencer polish.
- No on-screen text, no logos, no legible print, no reproduced journal cover typography. The journal, if present, is seen by edge, profile, open page at an oblique angle, or under a hand.
- No clinical scenes, no tests, no hospital, no exaggerated sadness.
- Loop: the final shot settles on a frame tonally close to the first.
- Desktop crop 16:9 with the left 45 % quiet enough for copy; mobile crop takes the centre-right 9:16 slice, so the subject sits right of centre.

## Concept A: field / human / lifecycle atmosphere (continuity with the old hero's emotional pull, widened beyond pregnancy)

1. 0 to 4 s. Wide. Golden late-afternoon meadow with tall seed-headed grass. A woman in a linen dress walks slowly away from camera, right of centre, one hand trailing the grass. Backlit. Slow lateral dolly left.
2. 4 to 7 s. Medium-close. At hip height a small child's hand holds an adult's hand as they walk through the same grass. Shallow focus, natural sway.
3. 7 to 10 s. Close. Wind moves through grass and wildflowers; the figures soft in the distance; light flares gently then settles. End frame tonally matches the first.

## Concept B: home / journal / intimate detail

1. 0 to 3 s. Morning window light across a wooden table. A cream linen landscape journal lies closed at the edge of frame, seen by its spine and edge only; steam rises from a mug; dust in the light. Very slow push in.
2. 3 to 7 s. Over the shoulder. Hands open the journal and begin writing with a pen; pages at an oblique angle so nothing is legible; focus on the hand's movement.
3. 7 to 10 s. Detail. A small photograph is slipped into a paper pocket on a page; a hand rests flat on the page. Settle.

## Concept C: mixed quiet-life progression with physical and digital continuity cues

1. 0 to 3 s. Dawn bedroom. A person sits by the window writing in a journal on their knees. Quiet, private. Locked-off.
2. 3 to 7 s. Kitchen, soft daylight. A visibly pregnant person leans against the counter, one hand on their bump, looking out of the window; on the counter beside them the journal lies closed next to a phone face-down. Slow push.
3. 7 to 10 s. Living room, afternoon. A parent with a newborn asleep on their chest; a toddler's hand reaches into frame to touch the baby's foot; the journal rests on the arm of the sofa. Settle on stillness.

## Model decision

Preflighted costs (16:9, silent, 10 s unless stated): Seedance 2.5 draft 480p 10 s = 30 credits (12 s = 36); Seedance 2.5 final 1080p 12 s = 192; Kling 3.0 Omni std multi-shot 10 s with image reference = 10; Kling 4.0 reference-to-video 720p 10 s = 5.

Seedance 2.5 was the expected first candidate, but one draft alone (30 credits) is close to the whole remaining authorised exploration allowance (35 credits) and three drafts (90) exceed it. Kling 3.0 Omni gives explicit ordered multi-shot prompts with per-shot durations (the exact need: coherent sequential storytelling with restrained motion), accepts image references for palette and material adherence, renders silent at no extra cost, and keeps all three concepts at 30 credits in total. It is therefore used for the draft round. Seedance 2.5 remains the recommended model for a finalised clip if the founder authorises the spend after review.
