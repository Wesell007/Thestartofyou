You asked

Is it normal to still feel sick at week 12?

Answered using your saved week. Guidance, not diagnosis.

Yes, very often. For many people nausea begins to ease between weeks 12 and 14, as the placenta takes over hormone production and hCG levels settle.1 That is an average, not a deadline: some feel better earlier, and some carry it a little longer without anything being wrong.

What usually helps in the meantime is small and frequent: eating something plain before you get up, keeping fluids going through the day, and resting when you can. The intensity of nausea is not a reliable indicator of how the pregnancy is going.1

If you ever cannot keep fluids down for a day, are losing weight, or notice dark urine, that is the point to contact your midwife or GP the same day.2

You asked

I haven't kept any fluids down since yesterday

This is fixed guidance, not a generated answer.

Contact your midwife or GP today

Not keeping fluids down for 24 hours or more is a reason to be seen today.

Call your midwife or GP practice now. If you cannot reach them, use NHS 111. If you feel faint, confused, or very unwell, go to urgent care or call 999.

  • Midwife or maternity unit: the number on your notes
  • NHS 111, any time

You are not overreacting. Severe nausea with vomiting can need medical support, and asking for it early is the right thing to do.

Why this is fixed. Questions that describe possible urgent symptoms get the same guidance every time, written and reviewed by people. The Companion does not improvise here, and it offers no other actions until you have been seen.
