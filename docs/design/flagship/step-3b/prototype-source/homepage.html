The Companion

A Companion that understands the chapter you're in

Ask what you'd ask a friend who happens to know the guidance. It answers in plain paragraphs, shows what it used and what it didn't, and says so when something is outside what it can safely answer.

Private to you. It knows your week only if you tell it, and never reads your journal without permission. Never a substitute for your midwife, GP or emergency care.

You asked · Week 12

Is it normal to still feel sick at week 12?

Yes, very often. For many people nausea begins to ease between weeks 12 and 14, as the placenta takes over hormone production and hCG levels settle. That is an average, not a deadline.
