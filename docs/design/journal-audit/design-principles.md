# Design principles, journal-owner inputs and proposed directions

Derived only from the audit (`physical-journal.md`, `packaging.md`). These are proposals for owner review; nothing is implemented.

## 1. Principles (each traced to evidence)

1. **A page, not a dashboard.** The journal organises everything as pages: a title, a small rule, then prompts and space. Digital surfaces should read as pages and chapters, not as grids of widgets. *(Every interior page; almost no boxed containers.)*
2. **Spaced capitals announce; plain sentences invite.** Titles are hairline display capitals with wide tracking; prompts are friendly sentence-case starters ending in "…". Keep the two voices distinct. *(Adorn Story Serif titles; Aileron prompts.)*
3. **Warm paper, not white screens.** Grounds are warm off-white and blush cream; text is near-black for legibility. *(`#F8F7F3`, `#FCF1E8`; text `#000000`.)*
4. **One quiet palette across the whole journey.** Pregnancy and the first year share the same cream, blush, sage and dusty blue. Stages are marked by chapter dividers, not by changing the colour scheme. *(The whole 148-page book holds one palette.)*
5. **Structure from rules, washes and whitespace.** Hairline lines, dotted title rules, soft watercolour washes and generous margins replace cards, shadows and gradients. *(Ruled lines, brush-swash prompt panels; no shadows anywhere.)*
6. **Nature at the edges.** Watercolour botanicals and woodland characters frame pages from the corners and never sit behind words; there are one or two per surface, and more only on chapter openers. *(Illustration placement throughout.)*
7. **Chapters with a ceremony.** Each stage opens with a calm divider spread. Digital transitions (new trimester, birth, first year) deserve a moment like that, not a toast. *(Section divider pages.)*
8. **Room to write is the product.** Generous space for the user's own words and photos takes priority over our content. *(The ratio of ruled space to printed copy.)*
9. **Warm, gentle, non-clinical; accurate where it matters.** Tone is encouraging; health information stays sourced and reviewed under TSOY rules. *(Reminders/top tips; health-content observations.)*
10. **Still by default; motion must earn its place.** Print is static. Digital motion should be rare and slow: a page turn, a chapter reveal. *(Contrast with the current 141 `animate-` uses.)*

*Considered and adjusted:* "premium without luxury-fashion" is **supported**, through restraint, hairline type and absence of badges, but the woodland watercolour characters make the brand **warmer and more storybook** than a minimalist luxury aesthetic. The directions below handle that balance differently.

## 2. Journal-owner experience inputs (design input only)

| Moment | Cue from the physical product |
|---|---|
| **Journal-owner welcome** | the cover composition: a centred spaced-caps "THE START OF YOU", a small rule, a quiet subtitle, woodland art at the corners on a blush-cream ground |
| **Activation screen** | the "story of you" page: a title plus a calm form-like layout (name/due date lines) and one action; no marketing |
| **"Your journal continues here"** | the founders' letter tone ("a safe space for you…"): a short warm paragraph, then the first prompt |
| **"In your journal this week"** | the weekly template: week title, then the page's prompts (memories and milestones, how I feel…) mirrored digitally, with "write it in your journal" or "add a photo here" choices |
| **Journal ↔ pregnancy linking** | the "Week N" and trimester-divider structure gives the mapping key (the journal week matches the journey week) |
| **Physical/digital bridge** | polaroid frames with washi tape → digital photo, voice and video slots styled as frames; prompts mirrored |
| **Contextual QR experiences** | per-page targets (a week page, a checklist, a mood board) → the digital equivalent of that page |
| **Owner-specific Companion cues** | the prompt voice ("…") lets the Companion offer "there's a space for this in your journal this week" without selling |
| **App Journal** | the monthly diary and firsts pages → an app Journal tab with the same chapters (trimesters, birth, months, first times) |

## 3. Three proposed visual directions (for owner review; not built)

### Direction A: "The Journal, Digitised" (faithful)

- **Core idea:** the website and app feel like opening the physical journal: each screen a page, each stage a chapter.
- **From the journal:** a direct translation of the divider spreads, title-plus-rule headers, watercolour washes behind prompts, polaroid photo frames and corner woodland art.
- **Typography:** the display serif (Adorn Story Serif, or a licensed equivalent) in spaced capitals for all titles; a neutral grotesque for prompts and UI; a text serif for long reading.
- **Colour:** cream `#F8F7F3` ground, blush for chapter openers, dusty blue-green for titles, sage and blush washes, near-black text.
- **Photography:** real journal product photography; user photos in polaroid frames.
- **Botanicals:** woodland watercolour characters per chapter (fawn, rabbits, squirrel) at the edges.
- **UI character:** square-ish, ruled, almost container-free; controls as text links and hairline buttons.
- **Companion:** presented as a page of the journal, with replies set like written entries.
- **Journal owner:** the digital week page mirrors the printed week page one to one.
- **Strengths:** the strongest product coherence; owners recognise it instantly; the clearest differentiation from SaaS.
- **Risks:**
  - can feel like a scrapbook or "skeuomorphic";
  - illustration rights and file weight;
  - heavy watercolour may lower legibility and accessibility;
  - may read as twee to non-owners and to TTC/IVF users, who are not yet pregnant.

### Direction B: "Editorial Keepsake" (refined)

- **Core idea:** a calm editorial publication with keepsake moments. The journal's typography and palette lead; illustration is used sparingly.
- **From the journal:** the spaced-caps titles, dotted rule, cream and blush grounds, ruled structure and the prompt voice; watercolour only at chapter openers and keepsake moments.
- **Typography:** the display serif for titles and chapter openers; a refined text serif for articles; the grotesque for UI and prompts.
- **Colour:** cream and white-cream grounds; the dusty blue-green as the single signature accent; sage and blush as quiet tints; brown reserved for small labels.
- **Photography:** calm, natural-light real moments and real product shots (to be commissioned or curated; Higgsfield only for supporting campaign assets).
- **Botanicals:** a single corner sprig or eucalyptus confetti; woodland characters only on chapter dividers and owner moments.
- **UI character:** an editorial grid, generous whitespace, hairline rules, small radius, quiet buttons, no shadows or gradients.
- **Companion:** a clean reading column with sources shown as footnotes; journal-style prompts as suggestions.
- **Journal owner:** a distinct but restrained owner layer: the "In your journal this week" band, chapter ceremonies, frames for photos.
- **Strengths:** balances premium and warmth; best for SEO, editorial and health-trust surfaces; scales to TTC, IVF, Toddler and Family; strong accessibility.
- **Risks:** could drift toward generic "calm editorial" if the journal cues (spaced caps, dotted rule, washes) are diluted; needs disciplined art direction.

### Direction C: "Woodland Companion" (warm, character-led)

- **Core idea:** the woodland characters become guides through the journey; warmth and storybook comfort lead.
- **From the journal:** the fawn, rabbits, squirrel and robin as chapter companions; a richer use of watercolour fields; the cover's layered blush and sage shapes.
- **Typography:** the display serif for titles; a softer grotesque for UI; the script used rarely for personal sign-offs ("Dear bump").
- **Colour:** fuller use of blush, peach and sage fields from the cover, with brown and dusty blue accents.
- **Photography:** minimal; illustration leads, with photography for the product and user memories.
- **Botanicals:** prominent; characters appear at milestones (a squirrel for the first year, rabbits at birth).
- **UI character:** soft shapes derived from the watercolour fields; gentle motion (falling leaves, page turns) used rarely.
- **Companion:** a gentle character-framed presence. Character avatars must never imply clinical authority.
- **Journal owner:** strongest emotional continuity with the cover and box.
- **Strengths:** memorable and emotionally warm; distinctive in the category; good for the app and keepsake moments.
- **Risks:**
  - risks childishness;
  - weakens clinical trust on health content;
  - poor fit for loss, IVF and TTC sensitivity;
  - depends on illustration rights and on a consistent supply of new art (a Higgsfield or illustrator pipeline);
  - motion and accessibility load.

**Recommendation for owner discussion:**
- **B is the base system** for all surfaces;
- **A's page and prompt language** is used for journal and owner surfaces (My Week, the digital journal, activation);
- **C's characters** are reserved for chapter openers and keepsake moments.

The owner decides.

## 4. Next step (plan only)

Use Taste to gather and critique external references **against these three directions**: editorial publications, premium journaling, calm health products, and keepsake apps. Record likes, dislikes and anti-patterns (`docs/design/references/`). Then choose a direction and design the flagship screens. Higgsfield comes later, for supporting visual assets only.
