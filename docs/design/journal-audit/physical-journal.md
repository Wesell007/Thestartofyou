# Physical journal audit: *The Start of You — One Year Pregnancy Journal*

Source: the journal print PDF (148 pages). Evidence labels as defined in `README.md`.

## 1. What the product is

- **Title, from the cover (VISUAL/FILE):** **THE START OF YOU**, with the subtitle **ONE YEAR / PREGNANCY JOURNAL**. The spine reads "THE START OF YOU · ONE YEAR PREGNANCY JOURNAL".
- **Scope:** pregnancy **and** the baby's first year. The letter (p. 2) says: "from the start of your pregnancy to the first year of your baby's life".
- **Legacy brand layer:** **kidproducts** ("all your child needs") appears on the back cover (p. 147), the box lid and the letter signature. **LEGACY / TRANSITIONAL PHYSICAL BRANDING.** Kidproducts is the old brand; The Start of You is the future consumer master brand. The current journal and packaging still carry Kidproducts only because the rebrand has not yet been rolled out and the The Start of You trademark process is still awaiting approval (founder-provided fact; no claim is made about the outcome). Kidproducts is not part of the future TSOY digital identity and is not a design input. The journal remains a design source for colour, typography character, spacing, hierarchy, prompts, illustration, keepsake feeling and structure; its Kidproducts layer is excluded.
- **Orientation:** every interior page is **landscape** (FILE: interior pages 416.1 × 274.7 mm in the PDF; covers 384.9 × 278.3 mm; spine 40.8 × 277.4 mm).
  - The PDF dimensions look like a design canvas rather than the trim size: body type is set at 17–19 pt in the file, so the file is probably scaled.
  - **Trim size, page count as bound, binding and paper weight: NEEDS PHYSICAL CONFIRMATION.**

## 4. Layout

- **Grid:** landscape page, typically **two columns** with a wide central gutter, or a single centred block for writing and photo pages.
  - Week pages alternate the column order between facing weeks: odd weeks place the baby update on the right, even weeks on the left (VISUAL, pp. 38–48).
- **Margins:** generous outer margins; the title sits well clear of the top edge; ample whitespace between prompt groups.
- **Alignment:**
  - titles centred;
  - prompts left-aligned within their columns;
  - "Firsts" and "Self care" pages use centred grids of 2 × n panels.
- **Rules and borders:**
  - hairline ruled writing lines (black/grey, 0.5–1 pt in-file);
  - thin table rules;
  - **rounded-corner outline "pill"** only for the "Your baby is the size of:" panel.
  - Otherwise there are almost no boxed containers: structure comes from rules, washes and whitespace.
- **Balance:** symmetrical on dividers and quote pages; asymmetric two-column balance on content pages.
- **Pacing:**
  - each section opens with a full-bleed **divider spread**: a large spaced-capital title, a small rule and one illustration;
  - then a predictable rhythm of prompt pages and photo pages;
  - "Notes" closes each section.

## 5. Botanical and illustration language

- **Style:** **naturalistic watercolour** woodland and botanical illustration, not line art: fawn/deer, rabbits, squirrel, robin, pine and fir trees, mushrooms, eucalyptus, olive leaves, lily, sunflower, autumn leaves, a cut log, butterflies.
- **Placement:**
  - at the **edges and corners** (bottom-left, bottom-right, top-left foliage), cropped by the page edge;
  - **never behind text**;
  - one or two motifs per page;
  - larger hero motifs only on covers and dividers.
- **Density:** sparse on content pages, richer on covers and dividers.
- **Recurring motifs:** eucalyptus leaves scattered as "confetti"; woodland animals as section characters (squirrel = first year, rabbits = birth, fawn = expecting); butterflies as small accents.
- **Non-botanical graphics:**
  - watercolour **brush-swash** panels (sage and blush) behind prompts and photo spaces;
  - **polaroid frames with washi tape**;
  - heart-shaped panels (self care);
  - a clothesline with pegs;
  - hand and foot prints;
  - a camera icon as the photo placeholder;
  - **photographic** fruit and vegetable cut-outs for "Your baby is the size of" (a different visual register from the watercolour art).

## 7. Prompt design and voice

- **Prompt form:** first-person sentence starters ending in an ellipsis ("My first indication that I could be pregnant…", "How I feel about my appearance right now…", "You make me laugh when…"). The voice moves from "I/we" during pregnancy to "you" addressed to the baby afterwards.
- **Writing spaces:** 3–6 ruled lines per prompt; full-page ruled pages for letters ("Dear bump", "Story of your birth", "My birthday wishes to you").
- **Weekly page template** (pp. 38–89):
  - week title;
  - **BABY UPDATE** (factual development note);
  - a photo space;
  - bump measurement;
  - **REMINDER** or **TOP TIP**;
  - prompts (memories and milestones, exciting events, how I feel about my appearance);
  - a highlights / challenges / cravings / dislikes table;
  - the "Your baby is the size of:" pill.
- **Monthly template** (first year): a "Favourites of the month" table, a photo, milestone, thoughts, and a "My monthly diary" page of prompts.
- **Checklists:** square tick-boxes, grouped by category (hospital bag, newborn, trimester to-do).
- **Tone:** warm, celebratory, gently reassuring ("You're doing great on this journey!"). Second-person encouragement inside reminders.

**Content observations to carry into digital (not design defects):**
1. **Health content.** The weekly baby updates, reminders, trimester to-dos and "Post birth reminders" (Apgar, newborn colour, the uterus) are health statements. Any digital reuse must go through the TSOY health-content and grounding rules (AGENTS.md; `docs/ai/article-grounding-governance.md`): sourced, reviewed, non-alarmist.
2. **"Gender guessing game"** (p. 14): presents folk tests (garlic test, carrying high or low, cravings) as playful predictors. Digital must **not** reproduce these as factual predictions.
3. **Gendered and family-structure language:** "Dear Mother", Mother/Father columns, "How your father is feeling", "My thoughts on becoming a new mother/father". The digital product supports co-parents and varied families, so wording needs an inclusive variant; this is an owner decision.
4. **Quotes attributed to "Winnie The Pooh"** (pp. 4, 145): attribution and rights need checking before any digital reuse.
5. **Spelling in the file:** "Pegnancy vitamins" (p. 37), "kegal" (pp. 37/53/73), "preperation" (p. 29). Note for a future reprint; not carried into digital.
