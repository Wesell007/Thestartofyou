# B. Premium journal / keepsake references

Looking for: memory products, storytelling, emotional permanence, photo treatment, writing and reflection experiences, meaningful empty space. Evaluated against the journal's prompt templates, polaroid frames, ruled lines and "room to write" (P8).

## B1. Day One (journal app)

- **Source:** App Store listing and Wikipedia ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/day-one-2-journal-notes/id1044867788), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_One_(app))); MacStories on its adoption of iOS Journaling Suggestions ([MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/apples-journal-app-journaling-for-all/)).
- **Exact screens:** the entry editor (text-first, photos inline, metadata captured automatically); **"On This Day"** (past entries resurfaced by date); the map and calendar views; templates for recurring journal types.
- **Works:** entry = text plus media plus quiet metadata, no card chrome; *On This Day* is a gentle, user-controlled memory resurfacing pattern; templates map well to TSOY's weekly and monthly prompt sets.
- **Does not work:** utility-app density in navigation; multiple journals and tags make it feel like a tool rather than a keepsake.
- **Translates:** the entry anatomy; "On This Day" as "this week last pregnancy / a year ago today" (only with loss-aware opt-out, see `health-wellness.md` C5); templates = journal page types.
- **Must not copy:** the feature breadth; the generic timeline list.
- **Principles:** supports P8; neutral on P1.
- **Direction relevance:** A strong (entry pages), B supportive.

## B2. Apple Journal (iOS 17.2+) and Journaling Suggestions

- **Source:** MacStories review ([link](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/apples-journal-app-journaling-for-all/)), The Sweet Setup first look ([link](https://thesweetsetup.com/our-first-look-at-apples-new-journal-app/)), Pratt design critique 2026 ([link](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2026/02/design-critique-journal-iphone-app/)), Whistleout ([link](https://www.whistleout.com.au/MobilePhones/News/apple-journal-app-review)).
- **Exact interaction:** starting a new entry presents **suggestions** drawn from photos, workouts, places and contacts, with per-data-type privacy controls; entries shown as cards on a softly coloured ground; reflection prompts offered as writable starting points.
- **Works:** the **suggestion-then-write** pattern lowers the blank-page barrier, which is exactly what the journal's "…" sentence starters do in print; privacy controls per data type are the right model for TSOY's permissioned memory (Gate 4, phase 44).
- **Does not work:** discoverability of the primary action (Pratt critique); card-on-colour entries contradict P5 (journal pages are not cards); lack of search/structure (Whistleout).
- **Translates:** prompt-first entry creation; "write it here or in your journal" as the TSOY variant; per-source permission for anything the Companion suggests.
- **Must not copy:** the card entry presentation; system-level data harvesting.
- **Principles:** supports P8, P2 (sentences invite); contradicts P5 in its entry visuals.
- **Direction relevance:** A strong, B supportive.

## B3. Qeepsake (text-message baby journal)

- **Source:** Trustpilot and App Store listing ([Trustpilot UK](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/qeepsake.com?page=2), [AppFollow listing](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/qeepsake/1332312787?country=ca)).
- **Exact interaction:** the service texts an age-based question; replying by SMS writes the entry; some questions are **deliberately repeated** so families see how answers change; entries print into books.
- **Works:** proves demand for *prompted, low-friction* capture during pregnancy and the first year; repetition-by-design is a strong keepsake idea ("the same question at 12, 24 and 36 weeks"); printed-book output closes the loop to a physical object.
- **Does not work:** users report repetitive or mistargeted prompts and slow editing; SMS is channel-locked.
- **Translates:** recurring prompts tied to journey week (the physical journal already repeats "Highlights / Challenges / Cravings / Dislikes" weekly); a future "print your year" bridge.
- **Must not copy:** SMS as the channel; pay-walled prompt packs.
- **Principles:** supports P8, P7 (recurrence as ceremony).
- **Direction relevance:** A strong.

## B4. Artifact Uprising (photo books)

- **Source:** 5280 ([link](https://5280.com/insta-book/)), Cool Hunting on the Layflat album ([link](https://coolhunting.com/buy/layflat-photo-album-artifact-uprising/)), Dealroom profile ([link](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/artifact_uprising)).
- **Exact product/screen:** book design studio with clean layouts and "no ornamental fonts or clip art"; minimalist fabric-covered albums; mission "off your device, into your life".
- **Works:** photo treatment is **plain and respectful**: generous margins, no filters, no stickers. The product photography shows real paper and cloth texture.
- **Does not work:** the brand is near-monochrome; a TSOY keepsake needs the journal's blush/sage warmth.
- **Translates:** photo presentation rules for the digital journal: full-bleed or white-margined, never filtered, never behind text; real product photography for the Journal page.
- **Must not copy:** the greyscale palette.
- **Principles:** supports P5, P6 (nothing behind text), P8.
- **Direction relevance:** B strong for imagery rules; A for the physical bridge.

## B5. Promptly Journals (prompted physical journals: Childhood History, Autobiography)

- **Source:** reseller listing and reviews ([Walmart listing](https://www.walmart.com/ip/17814914575), [Loox reviews](https://loox.io/s/NJgOjFNMSO), sponsored post at The House That Lars Built ([link](https://thehousethatlarsbuilt.com/?p=34879))).
- **Exact product:** linen-bound prompted journals covering pregnancy to age 18; four linen colours (Blush, Chambray, Wheat, Eggshell per the sponsored post); prompts praised as "well thought out… simple to answer".
- **Works:** the **closest direct competitor to the TSOY physical journal**: it proves the market for prompted, stage-structured keepsakes; its restraint (linen, no illustration) is the opposite pole to TSOY's woodland art and helps calibrate how much illustration is "too much".
- **Does not work:** no digital companion of note; sponsored coverage only.
- **Translates:** confidence that a long prompt structure (18 years) can stay coherent; the TSOY digital journal should keep its stage chapters.
- **Must not copy:** the linen palette or layout.
- **Principles:** supports P7, P8.
- **Direction relevance:** A/B calibration point for illustration density (C).

## B6. 1 Second Everyday (video memory timeline)

- **Source:** 1SE help centre ([link](https://help.1se.co/en/articles/966670-what-is-1-second-everyday)), TapSmart review ([link](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/1-second-everyday-review/)).
- **Exact interaction:** a calendar grid of days, one short clip per day, stitched into a film; "Freestyle" for arbitrary selections.
- **Works:** a **calendar-as-memory** overview is a strong app-Journal pattern; tiny daily capture lowers friction; the stitched film is a keepsake artefact.
- **Does not work:** the grid is utilitarian; paywalled clip length.
- **Translates:** a week/month calendar view in the app Journal where each cell shows a photo, voice or text entry; a "your trimester in a minute" keepsake export later.
- **Must not copy:** gamified completion of every day (pressure contradicts P9/P10 and loss sensitivity).
- **Principles:** supports P8; risk to P9 if streak-like.
- **Direction relevance:** A (app journal), B.

## Category conclusion

Keepsake references converge on three patterns TSOY should own: **prompt-first entry creation** (Apple Journal, Qeepsake, the physical journal itself), **plain respectful photo treatment** (Artifact Uprising), and **time-based resurfacing** (Day One's On This Day, 1SE calendar) with explicit opt-out for loss. None of them has a physical/digital bridge as strong as TSOY's journal could be, which is the differentiator to protect.
