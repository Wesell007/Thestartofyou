# G. Premium mobile product references

Looking for: Today/home surfaces, journaling, memory timelines, calm navigation, mixed text/photo/voice, small-screen typography. Evaluated against the future app (`docs/mobile/tsoy-native-app-roadmap.md`: Today · Journey · Companion · Journal · Family · You) and the journal's landscape spreads collapsing to a 390 px column.

## G1. Oura app (Instrument redesign)

- **Source:** Instrument case study ([link](https://www.instrument.com/work/oura-app)). *Engagement figures are the agency's own.*
- **Exact screen:** the **Today tab**: refined typography, a simplified component language, a unified semantic colour system, glanceable rings/bars with deeper trend views behind them.
- **Works:** one Today surface with a clear glance layer and a depth layer; semantic colour used for meaning, not decoration.
- **Does not work:** data-visualisation density and dark, techy register are wrong for TSOY; rings are a fitness idiom.
- **Translates:** TSOY Today = this week's page (glance: week title, one update, one prompt) with depth behind it (full week page, journal); semantic colour reserved for the urgency grading (C1), never for stage branding.
- **Must not copy:** rings, dark mode as identity.
- **Principles:** supports P1 (page), P4.
- **Direction relevance:** B (app Today), A (the page behind it).

## G2. Gentler Streak "For You" (cross-reference C3)

- A customisable, lighter home with suggestions rather than demands ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/03/18/gentler-streak-update-health/)). Translates to TSOY Today's tone: optional prompts, rest allowed, no streak pressure. Supports P9/P10.

## G3. Day One and Apple Journal on the phone (cross-reference B1, B2)

- **Exact screens:** Day One's timeline (text-first entries, inline photos, metadata), Apple Journal's suggestion sheet and entry composer.
- **Works at 390 px:** a single column of entries with generous leading; media inline, not in carousels; the composer opens from one obvious action (the Pratt critique shows what happens when it is not obvious).
- **Translates:** the TSOY Journal tab: a chapter list (trimesters, birth, months, first times) over a single-column entry stream; one primary "write / photo / voice" action with a text label.
- **Principles:** supports P8.
- **Direction relevance:** A.

## G4. How We Feel check-in (cross-reference C4)

- **Exact screen:** two taps to a named feeling, then an optional short note. A model for the TSOY feelings prompt on mobile: fast, word-based, optional journal field, no emoji grid.
- **Direction relevance:** A/B.

## G5. 1 Second Everyday calendar (cross-reference B6)

- **Exact screen:** month grid of captured days. Translates to a Journal month view where captured days show a thumbnail; empty days are simply empty (no "missed" styling, per loss and pressure rules).
- **Direction relevance:** A.

## G6. Reading apps and the typography floor (The Guardian app; GOV.UK scale)

- From `editorial.md` A1 and A5: headlines large but short-measured; **body never below 16–17 px on mobile**, measure around 45–60 characters, generous leading. Spaced-capital display titles must shrink or wrap deliberately at 390 px (a long tracked title wraps badly); the journal's two-word titles ("WEEK FOUR") are safe, but "APPROACHING MOTHERHOOD" needs a two-line treatment.
- **Direction relevance:** all; a constraint.

## What translates onto a 390 px screen (summary)

| Journal element (landscape spread) | Mobile translation |
|---|---|
| Title + dotted rule, centred | Title left or centred at a smaller tracked size, dotted rule kept short; two-line titles allowed |
| Two-column prompts | one column; prompts in document order; washes become full-width soft bands |
| Photo frames (polaroids with tape) | full-width framed photo blocks with a thin paper border; no rotation on mobile |
| "Size of" pill | one small inline row; **state-aware and opt-out** (loss rule) |
| Corner botanicals | a single small corner motif on chapter openers only; none on dense pages |
| Checklists | native-feeling rows with square ticks, hairline dividers, no cards |
| Divider spreads | full-screen chapter openers used at stage transitions (P7), swipe or tap to continue |

## Shared web/app patterns (recommended)

Type roles and scale, the cream/blush/sage/dusty-blue tokens, the title-and-rule header, the prompt block, the photo frame, the urgency-graded notice, the Companion answer column, the chapter opener. Navigation chrome differs (web masthead vs app tab bar) but both stay quiet: no pills, no blur bars.
